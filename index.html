Collect. Bind. Trade.

Characters drop in your group chats. Be first to bind one and it is yours.

DropsBindNex
  1. 01Add the bot to your group

    Open the bot, press Add Me and make it an admin so it can see messages.

  2. 02Chat normally

    A character drops every 110 to 500 messages, set by group admins with /setdrop.

  3. 03Bind it first

    Type /bind and the character's name. The first correct name keeps the character.

  4. 04Earn Nex and spend it

    Claim /daily, play /slot and /whoami, play Mines and Scramble here, then buy characters in the Shop.
