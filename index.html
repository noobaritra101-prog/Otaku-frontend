Guide

Start in two minutes.

Collect. Bind. Trade.

Characters drop in your group chats. Be first to bind one and it is yours.

  1. Add the bot to your group

    Open the bot, press Add Me and make it an admin so it can see messages.

  2. Chat normally

    A character drops every 110 to 500 messages, set by group admins with /setdrop.

  3. Bind it first

    Type /bind and the character's name. The first correct name keeps the card.

  4. Earn Nex and spend it

    Claim /daily, play /slot and /whoami, play Mines here, then buy cards in the Shop.
